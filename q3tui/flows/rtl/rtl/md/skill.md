A module whose file is generated by the APB-CSR-Generator (listed in `rtl/csr/provided.json`, header "Generated by the APB-CSR-Generator") is a given of the
design, not yours to write or change: instantiate it where the plan says, connect its APB bus ports to the bus ports of the design, its clocks and resets
to the matching ones, and its per-field ports (`i_<reg>_<field>` hardware inputs of `ro` fields, `o_<reg>_<field>` software-written outputs,
`i_hw_wdata_<reg>_<field>` / `i_hw_we_<reg>_<field>` hardware writes of `rwi` / `w1c` fields) to the logic that produces or uses those values. Read its ports in
its file; never leave a port unconnected without saying why.
